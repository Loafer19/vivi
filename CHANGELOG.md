# Changelog

## [Unreleased]

### Added
- **Bookmarks are saved and shown on the seek bar**: `Ctrl+B` (or the toolbar button) bookmarks the position of the file; bookmarks are kept per file in `~/.local/share/vivi/bookmarks.json` (keyed like mpv's watch_later) with a small frame for videos, drawn as accent pins on the seek bar and listed in the side panel's Bookmarks tab with time, name and note: click to jump, rename / add a note, delete. *Library → Playlists → Bookmarks* lists every bookmark and opens a file right there. They used to be forgotten on quit and only the last one could be removed. The control socket gets a `bookmarks` command.
- **Sleep timer: custom minutes, end of chapter, after N items, fade-out**: besides 15 / 30 / 60 min and end of file, the timer can run for any number of minutes, until the end of the chapter or for N queue items, and it lowers the volume over the last seconds (*Fade Out*, 30 s by default, `playback.sleep_fade`; Settings → Playback → Sleep timer) before pausing and restoring it. One timer serves the side panel, the top bar, the control socket (`sleep`, and `sleep` in `status`) and packages (`vivi-sleep <spec>`, host feature `sleep`, state in `user-data/vivi/sleep`).
- **Status indicators in the top bar**: while the sleep timer (moon + time left), loudness normalization, an A-B loop, a speed other than 1× or remote control is on, a small clickable accent chip says so and opens the matching panel or setting. Packages can add up to three chips with `vivi-indicator <id> <key> <json>` / `vivi-indicator-clear` (host feature `indicators`; clicks arrive as an `indicator` event on `vivi-ui`). New *moon*, *loudness*, *A-B loop* and *remote* icons (plain, two-tone and active).
- **Streams hub** (#86): **Library → Streams** gathers everything you stream. A URL bar plays a pasted link or searches radio (with the last 20 URLs one click away), and tabs show **Recent**, **Favorites**, **Radio**, **TV** and **Music** with the same cards and a ★ that works everywhere. Radio filters by country, genre, language, bitrate and top / new / trending; TV by country, group and program guide. Music switches between Free Music and music services such as YouTube Music; plugins join with the new `streams.music` contribution point. A shared now-playing card shows the station or title with ★, lyrics, record and download when they apply, and Home gets a *Streams* row (favorites and recent). Radio and TV come from the built-in Network Streams plugin and are hidden when it is turned off; URLs, Recent and favorites are core. `VIVI_PAGE=<place>`, the control socket's `page` command and new *Streams: …* palette entries open a tab or service directly.
- **Live plugin content on Home widgets and in the side drawer** (#83): packages fill their Home widget and drawer tab with live content (`vivi-content <id> [home|drawer|all] <json>`: a large value line, a sub line, a progress bar and rows with native bars and section headings; partial updates merge, `vivi-content-clear` drops it), open their drawer tab (`vivi-drawer <id> [open|close|toggle]`), put a native `bar` on plugin-dialog rows and open an info-only dialog (`"info_only": true`: no search row, no per-row play / queue). Vivi announces this as `user-data/vivi/host-api` = 1 and `user-data/vivi/host-features` (a word list: `content drawer bars info-dialog …`), so packages keep their old UI on older Vivi. The control socket's `drawer` command (and `VIVI_DRAWER=<id>` in screenshot mode) opens a tab without input synthesis. Documented in docs/plugins-architecture.md.
- **Downloads panel: a job's own "finished" wording**: a `vivi-download` report (or a built-in's `Report`) may set `done_title`, the heading of the notification when the job finishes, e.g. `"Export finished"`, so Cut & Export's clips notify "Export finished: clip.mp4" instead of "Download finished: …". Without it nothing changes; failures still say "Download failed", and older Vivi ignores the field. Documented in docs/plugins-architecture.md.

### Changed
- **Service dialogs are Library pages**: a source plugin's dialog (Free Music, Radio & TV, YouTube Music and other packages opened from their sidebar icon) opens as a full-width Library page with the now-playing card and a back arrow to Streams instead of a modal. Other plugin dialogs (Auto-tagger review, package settings) stay modal. Plugins need no change.
- **Stream favorites and history are core**: they move once from Network Streams' `saved.json` / `recent.json` (and the URLs of the global Recent list) to `favorites.json`, `history.json` and `urls.json` in `~/.local/share/vivi/streams/`; the old files are left as they were for older Vivi.

### Deprecated
- ***Open Network Stream*** (palette and player menu) and the Network Streams *Browse…* / palette entries are hidden aliases for one release: they open the Streams hub (the URL bar or the Radio tab), and so does the `VIVI_NETWORK` test hook (Radio tab). The aliases are removed in **0.15.0**; use *Streams: …*, IPC `page` or `VIVI_PAGE` instead.

### Fixed
- **Icons whose active and inactive states looked the same**: the library rail's *Recently played* and *Downloads* entries and the selected Settings category now show the solid accent icon like *Home* and the other rail entries (Settings no longer just tints the two-tone icon). New or redrawn active art for Recent, Downloads, About, Interface, Playback and Playback settings, which were missing or plain grey copies. The *Recording* category and the *Offline downloads* plugin icon get two-tone art, and the *Add file*, *Show in folder*, *Grid view* and *List view* menu rows are two-tone like the other menu rows. *Player bar icons: Single-tone* still draws them in one color.
- **Auto-tagger: Identify untagged songs while playing skipped songs in your library folders.** An untagged file's library row only repeats its file name, yet it counted as library metadata (now-playing `source` = `library`), so only untagged songs outside the library were identified. Such a row no longer counts: the source stays `file` and the song is fingerprinted like any other untagged one (also for the deprecated AcoustID package, which checks the same field).

## [0.13.1] - 2026-10-20

### Added
- **Close button in the player's top bar** (top right, after Fullscreen): closes the window like its title-bar button, so with *Close button: Minimize to tray* music keeps playing in the tray, with *Quit* Vivi exits.

### Changed
- **Two-tone icons everywhere they are drawn at 18 px and up** (#82): the control-bar buttons (play / pause, previous / next, ±10 s, volume at every level, fullscreen, pip, settings, more…), side-panel tabs, Settings categories, library rail and header, library tiles, Customize tiles, the ⋯ overflow menu, menu rows, Downloads actions, plugin icons in Extensions / Settings / the drawer, the Skip button and empty states now show the accent detail like the menus already did; small glyphs (16 px and less) stay single-tone. Primary (accent-filled) buttons draw both tones in the on-accent colour. 40 new or redrawn two-tone SVGs in the sheet style; `IconButton` has a `duo` mode and `TriIcon` takes an icon `name` (`ui/core/duo.slint`, generated by `tools/icon_layers.py`).
- **Player bar icons: Two-tone or Single-tone** (Settings → General → Appearance): the icons of the player's top bar (open, library, miniplayer, settings, fullscreen, close) and bottom bar (play / pause, previous / next, ±10 s, volume, toolbar items, plugin buttons, ⋯) are two-tone by default; *Single-tone* draws them in one color. Applies at once and is saved (`interface.bar_icons`); other icons stay two-tone and small 16 px icons are single-tone either way.

### Fixed
- **Windows build**: 0.13.0 did not compile for Windows (the exit cleanup removed the control socket, which exists only on Unix), so the release had no Windows zip.
- **Flatpak build**: the LuaJIT source was pinned to both the `v2.1` branch and a commit, so the build broke as soon as upstream `v2.1` moved on and 0.13.0 had no Flatpak; it is pinned to the commit only, like the Flathub manifest.
- **Free Music: the search box on Similar, album and artist pages** still showed the previous search, which was not what the page listed. Album and artist pages now clear it (the title names the page), *Similar* shows what it searches for, and *‹ Back* puts the earlier search text back.
- **Changelog**: two 0.13.0 fixes (*Plugin messages show when nothing plays*, *Music dialog no longer hangs with long lists*) had been copied into the Fixed list of every earlier release; they are listed under 0.13.0 only, and the plugin toast entries of 0.13.0 are one.

## [0.13.0] - 2026-10-20

### Added
- **Music Visualizer: colors from the album art** (*Colors → Album art* in the plugin's settings; *Theme accent* stays the default): the visuals take the main color of the playing song's cover (local files and music-service covers, from the shared now-playing track), adjusted to read well on the light and dark themes and eased in when the song changes. Covers without a clear color (black and white, grey) keep the theme accent.
- **Music Visualizer: Shift+V, command palette and control socket**: `Shift+V` goes back a mode (`V` forward, both in the shortcut lists); the palette has *Next / Previous visualizer mode*; `{"cmd":"visualizer","args":{"mode":"Radial"}}` on the control socket (name, index, `next` or `prev`; no mode just reports) and the vivi-mcp tool `set_visualizer` return the mode, all modes and whether it is showing.
- **Lyrics: click a line to jump there**: with synced lyrics of a file (or a music-service song), clicking a line in the karaoke view, the lyrics overlay or the side drawer seeks to it (with the track's timing offset); the drawer lines are buttons for screen readers ("Jump to this line"). Live radio keeps *click the line being sung to sync*.
- **Lyrics for music services and identified songs**: when an extension describes what it plays (`vivi-media-meta`, e.g. YouTube Music) or the Auto-tagger identifies an untagged song, Lyrics looks that song up on LRCLIB like a tagged file. A service's stream now plays as a normal synced karaoke from the song's start instead of the radio list; its offset and manual pick are kept per song. Lyrics from your pick, a `.lrc` file or the tags still win.
- **Lyrics: instrumental tracks**: a track LRCLIB lists as instrumental shows *Instrumental* (karaoke view, drawer, source label) instead of "No lyrics for this track", and is remembered for a month.
- **Equalizer handover from the deprecated Equalizer plugin**: Vivi sets `user-data/vivi/equalizer` = 1, so Equalizer 1.1 stops adding its own filter on top of the built-in one (both used to apply, doubling the boost), and the plugin can hand its preset over: `vivi-eq-preset <name>` selects a built-in or custom preset, `vivi-eq-import <json>` adds presets in the *Export* format (docs/plugins-architecture.md); both turn the equalizer on.
- **Auto-tagger: identify untagged songs while playing** (Vivi 0.13; *Identify untagged songs while playing* in the Auto-tagger settings, off by default): when a local song without title / artist tags (and not in the library or described by an extension) starts, it is fingerprinted in the background at low priority after a few seconds. When AcoustID is at least 70% sure, the identified title, artist, album and length show in the player, the now-playing card, MPRIS and to extensions (shared now playing, source `plugin:auto-tagger`), and a toast offers **Review tags**, the usual before/after review. Nothing is written to the file automatically. MusicBrainz is only asked when AcoustID has no title for the match, within the shared 1 request/s limit.
- **Auto-tagger: per-file result cache**: each file's AcoustID answer is remembered by path, size and modification time (at most 300 files, `~/.local/share/vivi/tagger-cache.json`, owner-only), so identifying a file again, playing it again or tagging its folder does not fingerprint it again; a retagged or re-encoded file is looked up anew. *Forget cached results* in the settings clears it. Answers the AcoustID package hands over (`vivi-tagger-acoustid`, still supported) are cached too.
- **AcoustID plugin deprecated**: the catalog marks it (1.1.1) replaced by the built-in Auto-tagger (`builtin:auto-tagger`); its Extensions tab and the one-time toast offer *Open Auto-tagger* (its settings).
- **A-B Loop & Frame Step plugin deprecated**: the catalog marks it replaced by the built-in keys (`builtin:ab-loop`); its Extensions tab and the one-time toast offer *Show shortcuts*.
- **Per-file subtitle offset in the player**: `Z` / `X` and now `Alt+Z` / `Alt+X` (`Shift`: 1 s), the side panel's Offset buttons and reset, and `set_subtitle_offset` work without the Subtitle Search plugin (it used to own the offset). Offsets stay in `subtitle-offsets.json`, so saved ones keep working; offsets the Subtitle Auto-Sync package kept itself on older Vivi are imported once at startup. Packages see `user-data/vivi/sub-offset` = 1 and can send `vivi-sub-offset-import` (docs/plugins-architecture.md).
- **Repeat and shuffle follow mpv**: Vivi's repeat button now follows `loop-file` whoever changes it (mpv keys, scripts, the Remote Control phone page, IPC), and shuffle is published as `user-data/vivi/shuffle` (`yes` / `no`; writing it shuffles or unshuffles the queue and the button follows), so extensions can offer repeat and shuffle without leaving Vivi's buttons out of date (docs/plugins-architecture.md).
- **Settings → Controls → Remotes → Phone remote control**: the placeholder *Web remote control* switch (it did nothing) is now a pointer to the Remote Control extension: *Install* (the usual Extensions → Browse flow with its permission prompt; enabled once installed), *Enable* when it is installed but off, *Settings…* (its Extensions tab) when it is on.
- **Shared now playing for extensions** (`user-data/vivi/now-playing`, docs/plugins-architecture.md "Now playing"): Vivi merges package metadata (`vivi-media-meta`), file tags, radio stream titles (ICY) and the library into one normalized track — title, artist, album, album artist, genre, track number, duration, cover, kind (music / video / podcast / stream), source, path, MusicBrainz id, service and a change counter — readable from Lua, mpv's JSON IPC and the control socket (`now_playing`). `user-data/vivi/now-playing-api` tells packages it is there. MPRIS (now also with album artist and track number, and cover art on every platform) and the now-playing card read the same track, so a radio title change or metadata a music plugin sends later shows up everywhere. `vivi-media-meta` takes optional `album_artist`, `mbid`, `duration`, `kind` and `service`.
- **Secret settings stay out of mpv's log**: a package with `secrets_file = true` gets its `secret` settings (API keys, tokens, session keys) from a private file (`plugin-data/<id>/vivi-secrets.json`, 0600) instead of mpv `script-opts`, which mpv writes to its log file with their values.
- **Plugin toasts on every screen** for as long as the plugin asks: toasts from extensions (e.g. a sign-in result) were only drawn over the player, so they were invisible in the library, the Extensions window or a plugin dialog; they now show on every screen and sit above every dialog (Customize Player, onboarding, Properties, shortcuts, lyrics search, tag review). `vivi-toast <text> <seconds>` is honored within 1.5–15 s (default 4 s).
- **Scene chapters in Chapters & Previews**: the Chapter Thumbnails package (now called Chapters & Previews, 1.2.0) includes scene detection, so *Video → Detect scene chapters* and the palette entry come from it; the deprecated Scene Chapters package keeps working alone, and its entries are hidden (menus, palette and its *Where it shows up* list) while Chapters & Previews offers the same.
- **One Skip button for intros, credits and extension segments**: Skip Intro & Credits now takes ranges from extensions (`vivi-skip-ranges <source> <json>`, `vivi-skip-ranges-clear`, `vivi-skip-undo`; packages check `user-data/vivi/skip-api`, docs/plugins-architecture.md), so SponsorBlock 1.3 segments show as *Skip sponsor* / *Mute self-promotion* on the same button instead of SponsorBlock's own toasts. A segment found by chapter name, a per-show range and an extension is one button. New settings: *Per category or source* (skip / ask / off, e.g. `intro=skip sponsorblock:filler=off`), *Offer to always skip for a show* (after a manual skip the button offers *Always skip the intro of this show*; kept in *Shows skipped automatically*, cleared by *Forget this show*). Automatic skips leave *Skipped intro · Undo* on the button for 6 s; `Alt+K` presses the button and `Alt+Shift+K` undoes the last skip or unmutes (also *Skip segment* / *Undo last skip* in the command palette). Chapters added after the file opened (SponsorBlock, scene detection) are picked up.
- **A-B loop and frame step keys** (from the A-B Loop & Frame Step plugin, which the core now replaces): `Alt+A` / `Alt+B` set loop point A / B at the playhead (set on the wrong side of the other point, the two swap, so the loop never runs backwards), `Alt+C` clears the loop, `Alt+←` / `Alt+→` move the point set last by one frame (container frame rate; it never crosses the other point), `,` / `.` step one frame back / forward while paused (1 s seek while playing). Feedback is a toast and the loop is drawn by the seek-bar A/B overlay, no mpv OSD bar. All are in the shortcut list and the command palette; the Alt keys work by physical key on any layout. `Shift+L` still cycles set A / set B / clear.
- **Music dialog for packages**: rows can carry `saved` (a check mark on the tile and row for items downloaded already) and the update `detail_cover` (a large cover above the detail text). Plugin icon keys `youtube-music`, `youtube` and `download` get their own glyphs (YouTube and Offline Downloads looked like Network Streams).
- **Playlists synced by extensions**: an extension can save a playlist that follows an online list (`vivi-playlist`, docs/plugins-architecture.md); YouTube Music's *Download playlist* uses it. Such playlists show the extension's name as a badge in Library → Playlists, and their menu has **Sync** and **Open Source**. A playlist you made is never overwritten: a clash gets "Name (YouTube Music)".
- **Grouped downloads**: a playlist download is one row in the Downloads panel ("12 / 40", failures, progress by tracks) with its tracks indented under it; a chevron shows all tracks, collapsed only the one downloading now is shown. Tracks finish quietly, the playlist notifies once.
- **Downloads panel**: Library → Downloads (with a badge for running jobs) lists every extension's downloads in one place: thumbnail, title, plugin, progress, size, speed and time left, with Pause / Resume, Cancel, Retry, Play now, Open file, Show in folder, Remove and Clear finished. The list is kept across restarts and finished / failed downloads notify you. Free Music's downloads show up there; extensions report theirs with `vivi-download` (see docs/plugins-architecture.md).
- **Offline Downloads** 1.1.x reports its jobs into the Downloads panel (progress, pause / resume, cancel, retry); the panel and the command palette get *Record stream* (records the playing stream, or a stream URL from the clipboard) and *Add URL from clipboard*.
- **Tray icon and background playback** (Linux, StatusNotifierItem): closing the window keeps music and radio playing with Vivi in the system tray. Left click shows / hides the window, middle click plays / pauses; the menu has the current title, Play / Pause, Previous, Next, Show / Hide, plugin entries (`tray.item`), active downloads and Quit, and the tooltip shows the title. Starting `vivi` again (or opening a file with it) brings the hidden window back and plays the file. Settings → General → Window → Close button: *Minimize to tray* (default) or *Quit*; without a system tray closing quits as before.
- **Desktop notifications from extensions** (`notify`): a system notification on Linux (`org.freedesktop.Notifications`) when a download finishes or fails; packages can send `vivi-notify`. Turn it off in Settings → Extensions → Options (messages then show in the player).
- **Equalizer shortcuts**: `Alt+E` selects the next equalizer preset (built-in, then your custom ones) and turns the equalizer on, `Alt+Shift+E` turns it on / off; a toast shows the preset. Both are in the keyboard shortcut list and the command palette.
- **Auto pre-amp / prevent clipping** in the Equalizer (Audio panel), on by default: the preamp is lowered by the largest band boost (never more than the preset already lowers it), so boosted presets do not clip; the hint under the bands shows the resulting gain. Saved in `fx.toml` (`auto_preamp`).
- **Bass, Vocal and Night equalizer presets** (from the Equalizer plugin, which the built-in equalizer now replaces).
- **Deprecated plugins**: a catalog entry can be marked `deprecated` (message, Vivi version, replacement). Such a plugin is no longer offered under *Available plugins* (still listed while installed), its Extensions tab shows the notice with a button to open the replacement, and a one-time toast with the same button appears the next time a file plays. The **Equalizer** plugin is the first: the built-in Equalizer (Audio panel) replaces it, and *Open Equalizer* opens it.
- **Plugin buttons in the bottom bar**: Customize Player lists buttons from your extensions (Karaoke lyrics view, Mark intro / credits, Search subtitles online, and any package that contributes `controlbar.button`) after the built-in items; none is on until you add it, and a button hides while its plugin is off.
- **Home widgets**: plugins that contribute `home.widget` get a card under *Widgets* on Home (Network Streams: Radio stations, Free Music: Discover free music); show / hide and reorder them from the plugin's gear view in Extensions.
- **vivi-mcp: more for AI agents** (docs/mcp.md): 13 new tools (47 in all): queue editing (`playlist_move`, `playlist_remove`, `playlist_clear`), repeat and shuffle, the Recent list (`list_recent`), saved playlists (list, open, save the queue), lyrics timing and the karaoke view, and plugin commands: `list_plugin_commands` / `run_plugin_command`, plus every command a plugin adds to the palette, menus, tray or control bar shows up as a tool of its own (`plugin_<id>_<command>`), so new plugins reach agents without changes to vivi-mcp. `screenshot` returns the frame as an image the agent can see (`save: false` keeps no file). `get_status` also reports chapter, audio track, repeat, shuffle and whether lyrics are loaded. New resources `vivi://recent`, `vivi://lyrics`, `vivi://plugin-commands`, with change notifications for every resource (`resources/subscribe`) and for the tool list. Prompts: `now_playing`, `play_radio`, `queue_from_library`, `fix_subtitles`. Every tool has a title and annotations (read-only / destructive / idempotent / open-world), so clients ask before clearing the queue or running plugin code; arguments are checked before they reach Vivi, list tools page with `offset` / `limit`, and results always carry structured content. Speaks MCP 2026-07-28 (stateless requests, `server/discover`, `subscriptions/listen`) as well as the handshake versions up to 2025-11-25.
- **Host API for downloadable plugins** (docs/plugins-architecture.md, "Host API for packages"): packages can open and fill the shared music dialog (rows with covers, list / cover grid, sources, chips, filters, details, actions, infinite scroll) and get the user's clicks back, show a confirm / warning dialog, describe the URLs they queue for the now-playing card and Recent covers, and make credential files in their own data folder private or wipe them. Covers from URLs go through the image cache.
- **Cover grid and keyboard navigation in the rich music dialog**: a list / grid toggle for plugins that offer it (square cover tiles with title, subtitle and badge); arrow keys, Home / End and Enter work in both views, with a focus ring and list items for screen readers. The licence chip is hidden when a row has none.
- A shared **confirm dialog** for plugins (warning badge, Cancel / confirm buttons, Enter confirms, Esc cancels).
- **Command palette** (`Ctrl+K`): type to fuzzy-search every player action (with its shortcut), Extensions / Customize / Open Network Stream, the sources and every plugin command (`palette.command`); Up / Down / Page Up / Page Down select, Enter runs, Esc closes. Rows are list items for screen readers. `Ctrl+K` used to be a second Play / pause key (`K` and Space still are).
- **Plugin side drawer** in the player: a right slide-out panel with a tab per plugin that contributes `drawer.panel` (Lyrics shows the synced lines with the current one highlighted; Network Streams its actions), a two-tone gear to its settings and a close button. It hides with the player controls and Esc closes it; open it from the player menu → Plugins → Side drawer or the palette.
- **Radio lyrics: click a line to sync**: on network streams the song's position is unknown, so lyrics now open as a scrollable list (no highlight) with a *click the line being sung to sync* hint. Clicking a line (or Tab to it and Enter / Space) takes its timestamp as the current stream time; from then on the karaoke view and overlay highlight and scroll normally, including the word wipe and shrink-to-fit of long lines. The existing offset control and `[` / `]` fine-tune it (only for this listen, disabled until synced), clicking another line re-syncs, and a new stream title starts the next song unsynced. Plain lyrics stay a list; files are unchanged.
- **Extensions window** (`Ctrl+E`, the plugin icon at the bottom of the sidebar rail, main menu → Plugins → Extensions…, or Settings → Extensions → Open Extensions): a vertical tab per plugin (icon + name; Sources and Features grouped) plus **All / Marketplace** for installing, updating, enabling and disabling (the catalog code from Settings, unchanged). A plugin tab shows what it does, its status, where it shows up and its actions (or **Open** for a source); its ON/OFF switch and a two-tone gear button sit in the tab header, and the gear switches to the plugin's settings. Up / Down move between tabs; Esc closes it through the overlay stack. Settings → Extensions is now a short page linking to it and keeps the global options (daily update check, load add-ons on startup, scripts folder, rescan).
- **Sources in the sidebar and on Home**: source plugins (Network Streams, Free Music, YouTube, and any plugin that declares `sidebar.source`) appear in the library's icon rail and as a **Sources** row on Home; a click opens the plugin's browser. Each source's settings (gear) can hide it or move it up / down.
- **Plugins in the menus**: plugin actions appear where they belong instead of only in Settings: *Search online…* (Subtitle Search) and *Auto-sync subtitles* in the Subtitle menu, the equalizer in the Audio menu, scene detection in the Video menu, *Auto-tag…* on library files and folders, and a **Plugins** submenu in the player's right-click menu (Find lyrics, Stream info, Download for offline, clip in/out, …).
- **Contribution points for plugins** (`src/plugins/contrib.rs`, docs/plugins-architecture.md): a manifest may set `role = "source" | "feature"` and `[[contributes]]` entries (`point`, `label`, `command`, `icon`) for `sidebar.source`, `menu.subtitles`, `menu.audio`, `menu.video`, `context.item`, `context.player`, `palette.command`, `drawer.panel`, `home.widget`, `controlbar.button`, `tray.item` and `notify`. Manifests without them keep working: catalog packages get built-in defaults by id, others are features shown in the Extensions window only.
- **Network Streams grid view**: the streams browser can show stations as a grid of logo tiles with the name under each logo (two lines, then elided; the full name in a tooltip), as an alternative to the list. Two-tone List / Grid buttons in the dialog header switch it, and the choice is remembered as the plugin's **Browser view** setting. In the grid, logos are decoded at tile size (256 px) through the shared image cache.
- **Keyboard navigation in the streams browser**: in both views the arrow keys move the selection (Up / Down move a row in the grid), Home / End jump to the first / last station, Enter plays, and the selection is scrolled into view. The results area is in the Tab order with a focus ring, and rows and tiles are list items with the station name for screen readers.
- **Subtitle Search: episode and year aware search**: a query with an episode (`Show S01E05`, `s1e5`, `Show 1x05`) or a release year (`Movie (2020)`, `Movie 2020`) is sent to OpenSubtitles as the title plus `season_number` / `episode_number` or `year` instead of one text string, so episode files find that episode instead of the whole show; when that finds nothing the plain text is searched again. A year alone (`1917`) stays a title. Also for `search_subtitles` over MCP.
- **Subtitle Search: Load exact matches automatically** (off by default; checkbox *Auto-load exact matches* in the Add Subtitles dialog, also in the plugin settings): when a local video opens without a subtitle track in your languages (embedded tracks in other languages do not count; external files and unlabelled tracks do) and no remembered pick, Vivi looks it up by its hash in the background and loads the most downloaded exact match (not machine translated; hearing-impaired only when there is nothing else), with a toast. One download per new file, tried once per file and session, and remembered as the file's pick like a manual *Load*.
- **Free Music: downloaded tracks** are marked with a check in the results (the file is in the download folder), and Play / Queue / double-click play that file instead of streaming it again (offline, with the tags and cover written at download time); a finished download gets its check mark right away.
- **Free Music: licence and cover while you choose and play**: selecting a track shows its large cover above the details (licence, what it allows, attribution, source page), and a playing Free Music song shows its source and Creative Commons licence on the now-playing card's info line (e.g. *Jamendo · CC BY-SA 3.0 · MP3 · 147 kbps*). *Web page* is now *Open on Jamendo* / *Open on Internet Archive*.

### Changed
- **New app logo** (a rounded play triangle; window, tray, About and package icons), also shown on the onboarding welcome step and the empty library (*Your library is empty*) instead of the VLC-style cone.
